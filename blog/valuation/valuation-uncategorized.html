Relative Valuation (Part 1)

The Basics

  • Relative valuation involves valuing assets based on the way similar assets are being priced in the market

Steps to Relative Valuation

  1. Find comparable assets that are priced in the market
  2. Scale the market prices to a common variable to standardize prices
  3. Adjust for differences across assets
