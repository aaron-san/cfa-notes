## Financial Statement Analysis (cont.)

### Mergers and Acquisitions

#### Consolidating Income Statements
1. Eliminate intercompany revenues and expenses from intercoompany transactions including:
  - Intercompany sales
  - Related COGS
  - Interest income or expenses on intercompany loans
  - Management fees or other costs charged by the parent and paid by the subsidiary
