## Portfolio Management


### Styles
1. Value style (heavy value stocks)
2. Growth style (heavy growth stocks)
3. Core style / Market-oriented (balanced) (e.g., with value/growth bias)

### Sharpe Ratio
- Used to measure the contribution of an asset to a portfolio or portfolio overall performance
- Use when portfolio is partially invested (idiosyncratic risk exists)

### Treynor Ratio
- Used to measure the contribution of an asset to a portfolio or portfolio overall performance
- Use when portfolio is fully invested (only systematic risk exists)
